Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Orbis ("the App") is a Reversi game for iPhone and iPad, played on a globe and sacred-geometry boards. This policy describes how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

None. Orbis does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App runs entirely on your device with no server component.

Saved Settings & Game Progress

Your preferences, chosen difficulty, and tutorial progress are stored on your device only. They are not accessible to us or any third party.

In-App Purchase

Orbis offers an optional one-time "Orbis Pro" purchase that unlocks the additional board shapes, handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the transaction; we never receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. Restoring a purchase relies solely on your Apple Account entitlement as provided by Apple.

On-Device AI Opponent

The computer opponent runs entirely on your device. No game data, moves, or board states are recorded, uploaded, or shared. The App works fully offline.

Analytics & Tracking

Orbis contains no analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, no ad networks, and no third-party code that collects user data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs. The only Apple framework that leaves the device is StoreKit, used solely to complete and restore your purchase.

Children's Privacy

Orbis does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we collect no data from any user, this is inherently satisfied.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new date. Since we collect nothing, changes are unlikely.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Wall Rats ("the App") is developed by Klimb. This policy describes how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

None. Wall Rats does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App runs entirely on your device with no server component.

Camera & AR Data

Wall Rats uses your device camera, LiDAR scanner, and ARKit to display augmented reality content. Camera frames and spatial mapping data are processed locally on your device in real-time. This data is never recorded, uploaded, or shared with anyone — including us.

Saved Configurations

Your wall design settings are saved locally on your device only. They are not accessible to us or any third party.

Video Recording

When you use the screen recording feature, videos are saved to your device's photo library via Apple's ReplayKit framework. We have no access to these recordings.

Analytics & Tracking

Wall Rats contains no analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, no ad networks, and no third-party code that collects user data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

Children's Privacy

Wall Rats does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we collect no data from any user, this is inherently satisfied.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new date. Since we collect nothing, changes are unlikely.
